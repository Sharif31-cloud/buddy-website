FAQ

Is Buddy Anti-Theft and SignalHawkBot safe?

Buddy Anti-Theft only collects device info necessary for security alerts. SignalHawkBot only communicates with Firebase for subscription verification. No personal or trading credentials are transmitted.

Why do I see a warning when downloading the Buddy Anti-Theft app?

Because the app is not downloaded directly from the Google Play Store, Google may show a standard warning before installation. This is a normal security precaution for all apps distributed outside the Play Store. The app is safe to install and does not contain any viruses or harmful software.

Can I trust this bot with my trading account?

The bot only reads credentials locally to connect with MetaTrader 5. Credentials are encrypted and never sent to any server other than subscription verification via Firebase.
